Wake Instrument
PIL-WK-01
A Wake reading is not a document.
It is a notation.
The Wake is the structural residue left by traversal across time — what the work holds that the practitioner did not put there deliberately.
The instrument does not produce understanding, coherence, or narrative.
It produces visibility under constraint.
The instrument reduces interference between the practitioner and what the work is already doing. It holds the boundary between what is present, what is added, and what is wanted.
It does not interpret the work. It prevents the practitioner from converting residue into explanation.
Entry conditions
You may proceed only if you will not use this instrument to confirm what you already believe, to understand yourself, or to produce meaning, coherence, or narrative.
If any of those fail, stop. The form cannot be used from that position.
You agree to remain with what is present without adding, resolving, or interpreting. Every collapse into explanation is interference, not insight.
You agree not to convert recognition into understanding, pressure into narrative, residue into biography, or structure into intention. Conversion is collapse. Collapse voids the reading.
The entry conditions can be stated. They cannot be verified. A practitioner may accept them and proceed in violation. No instrument can prevent this. The instrument does what an instrument can do. The rest is on the practitioner.
Eligibility condition
The Wake can only be read when the work has accumulated long enough to have left something the practitioner did not put there deliberately.
The work knows something the practitioner does not. That is the only eligibility condition.
