Note: Read Before Proceeding

This document operates as a reading instrument. If it appears complete, the reading has closed before the document has.

The distinctions established here are intended to remain valid beyond this document.

It does not interpret the work. It recreates the conditions under which the work can be correctly read. Three kinds of statement appear throughout: archive (what is documented), practice definition (how the layers and instruments operate), and interpretation (what the archive suggests). They are visually distinct. Read them accordingly.

01. Source
The Origin Event

Archive

May 2007. A peak experience that preceded the naming of the practice by several years. Project 5am was established in 2007. Its name draws from Allen Ginsberg's poem "Five A.M." The 2014 studio concept map (produced during the Flux Without Pause graduation period) shows PIL's diagnostic principles operating as conscious studio positions before PIL was named. Jason Kofi-Haye used the name "Quixote" before Project 5am was established.

Practice Definition

Project 5am operates as the field layer of the practice. Its conceptual anchor is Rumi's field poem: "Out beyond ideas of wrongdoing and rightdoing, there is a field. I'll meet you there." That field is the post-instruction space: the condition PIL names and works within.

Interpretation

The 2007 event functions in the archive less as a beginning than as a disclosure point. The distinction matters: a beginning produces what follows it; a disclosure reveals what was already present. A beginning can be identified at the moment it occurs. A disclosure is only confirmed by subsequent evidence: this is why the 2014 concept map is load-bearing here. It documents a formation operating seven years after the event and twelve years before the framework, without the benefit of either. The outputs that follow 2007 are best understood as deposits from that event rather than developments beyond it, though this reading remains interpretive and should be tested against the archive rather than assumed from it.

Archive: Name History

"Quixote" was the identity carried before Project 5am. The shift is documented in the archive as a the naming of the condition rather than a rebranding. PIL was retrospectively identified as the framework that diagnosed what "Quixote" was living through. The "No to £650" petition (509 signatories) RA-06 is documented as the earliest transmission event in which the structural logic of the practice became visible to others without being labelled by Jason. The 2014 studio concept map RA-04 is primary archive material for this period.
